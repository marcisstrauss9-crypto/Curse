Visit Riga

Enjoy the local culture and traditions while visiting interesting places.

Riga Old Town

Riga's Old Town is a charming cobblestone labyrinth filled with centuries-old Gothic churches, vibrant squares, and picturesque medieval architecture.

Central Food Market

Spanning five historic Zeppelin hangars, Riga Central Market offers an immersive culinary journey packed with authentic local delicacies and buzzing market energy.

River Boats

Set sail on the River Daugava aboard the modern VECRĪGA for a memorable 1-hour panoramic tour.
